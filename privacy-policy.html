Privacy Policy

Hydriva

A water & vitamin reminder app that keeps everything on your phone.

  • Data collectedNone
  • Account requiredNone
  • Servers / cloud syncNone
  • Ads & analyticsNone

Last updated July 3, 2026

Summary

Hydriva works entirely on your device. There is no account, no backend server, and no cloud sync — your check-ins and reminder settings never leave your phone.

What's stored, and where

Hydriva saves your daily water and vitamin check-ins and your reminder preferences (times and days) in your device's local app storage. This data is not accessible to the developer and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions Hydriva requests

Hydriva asks for notification permission so it can show local reminders — like "Time to hydrate" — at the times you set in Settings. These are scheduled entirely on your device; Hydriva does not use push or remote notifications, and no notification data is sent to a server.

Third parties

Hydriva does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not share data with any third party — because it does not collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

Hydriva does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hydriva changes what's described here — for example, by adding an optional feature that stores data outside your device — this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
